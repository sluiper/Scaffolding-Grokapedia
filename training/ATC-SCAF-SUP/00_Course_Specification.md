# ATC-SCAF-SUP — Supervisor / Team Leader

**Status:** 0.2-draft · **Duration:** 2 days provisional  

## Intent
Supervise scaffold teams: planning, PTW interface, competency gate, SIMOPS, stop-work, client/Anabeeb rules.

## Entry
Prefer BASIC + field experience; site supervisor role.

## Pass
Theory ≥80%; leadership practical checklist all critical.

## Four gates (modelled on HPWJ SUP pattern)

1. **People** — only competent scaffolders erect/modify  
2. **Plan** — RA/JSA, duty class, design if needed  
3. **Permit & tag** — PTW, inspect/approve, correct tag  
4. **Protect** — drop zone, WAH, weather, SIMOPS
