# ATC-SCAF-AWARE — Student Handout  
## نشرة المتدرب

## Your job as a scaffold **user**

1. Check the **tag at every access, every time** before you climb.  
2. **Green** — safe for intended use (still check condition).  
3. **Yellow** — restricted — **harness & lanyard mandatory**.  
4. **Red** — **do not use** — scaffolders only.  
5. Missing/damaged tag → **do not use** → tell supervisor.  

## Never

| Never | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Modify scaffold (move boards, remove rails/ties) | Only scaffolders — MCR-060 |
| Enter barricaded modify zones | Incomplete structure — MCR-060 |
| Carry materials while climbing ladder | Fall risk — MCR-061 |
| Move scaffold with forklift/machine | Collapse/shift risk — MCR-062 |
| Overload platform / store bulk materials without design | Collapse — MCR-015/054 |

## Ladder

- Slope about **4 up : 1 out**  
- Extends **≥ 1 m or 3 rungs** above landing  
- Three-point contact; firm base  

## Dropping things kills

- Tool lanyards as required  
- Respect barricades under overhead work  

## If unsure

**Stop work / ask supervisor.** Tag + permit + MCR beat memory.
