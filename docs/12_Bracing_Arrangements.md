---

## 1. Why brace

Bracing provides **lateral stiffness** so the scaffold does not rack or collapse sideways under wind, dynamic use, or imperfect geometry.

## 2. Angle & node rules

| Rule | Value | MCR |
|------|-------|-----|
| Brace angle from horizontal | **35°–55°** | 016 |
| Connection | **Nearest to node points** | 016 / deck s57 |

**Bad:** far from nodes; angle outside band. [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s57]

CISRS/TG20 training quizzes often use **≤ 300 mm** from node — **not** adopted into MCR until primary/TG20 citation attached (secondary only).

## 3. Independent scaffold density

| Brace | Rule | MCR |
|-------|------|-----|
| Face bracing | Both ends + every **5th** line of standards | 020 |
| Ledger bracing | Alternate lines of standards | 020 |

Façade patterns (deck s42): single / double / continuous façade bracing.

## 4. Free-standing & mobile

- Brace **all four sides**.  
- Free-standing plan braces: **top, kicker, every third lift** (**MCR-057**).  
- Mobile: plan brace bottom, top, intermediate lifts (deck s40).  

## 5. Couplers for braces

Swivel couplers for non-90° brace connections (**MCR-009**). Prefer correct class B fittings for load paths.
