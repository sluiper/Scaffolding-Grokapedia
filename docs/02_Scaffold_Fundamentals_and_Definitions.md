## 1. What a scaffold is

A scaffold is a **temporary safe platform for elevated work** or a **temporary safe structure for elevated work**. [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s5]

It is temporary works equipment — not permanent structure. Design, erection, inspection, use, modification, and dismantling are controlled activities.

## 2. Who is a scaffolder

A **scaffolder** is a competent and certified person who erects scaffold. [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s5]  
Only scaffolders may modify scaffold (**MCR-060**). End users must not.

## 3. Four activity phases

Scaffold operations run in sequence [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s5]:

1. **Transportation** (mobilisation / demobilisation) of materials  
2. **Erection**  
3. **Modification**  
4. **Dismantling**  

Each phase has different fall and collapse risk profiles (partial structure, incomplete edge protection, open drop zones).

## 4. Major risk classes

| Risk | Typical mechanisms | Primary control families |
|------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| Fall from height | Incomplete platform, missing guardrails, bad access, yellow-tag misuse | MCR-021, 023, 028–030, 046, 049 |
| Collapse | Overload, missing ties/bracing, bad foundations, wrong couplers | MCR-015/054, 008–014, 016–020, 033 |
| Struck-by / drop | Tools, materials, gin wheel loads | MCR-031, 047, 048, 061 |
| Same-level slip/trip | Debris, incomplete boards, weather | MCR-051, housekeeping |

Scaffolding is among the highest-risk construction occupations; annual fatalities and injuries remain material. [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s5 — qualitative training statement]

## 7. Temporary platform context (Anabeeb WAH)

Where permanent platforms are unavailable, temporary options include scaffolding, mobile scaffolding, suspended scaffolding, hop-ups, personnel cages, portable stepladders (**MCR-052**). Fall protection applies at free-fall risk **≥ 1.8 m** (**MCR-046**).

## 8. Engineering gate

Load-bearing, bridge, special, cantilever **> 3 m** (**MCR-058**), or configurations outside standard compliance sheets require competent design review (**MCR-045**).
