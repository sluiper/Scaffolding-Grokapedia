## 1. Why erect/modify/dismantle is different

During progressive build, **collective edge protection is incomplete**. Risk of fall and drop is highest. Hierarchy:

1. Collective protection as soon as practicable (guardrails, decks).  
2. Work methods that keep workers behind completed edges.  
3. Personal fall arrest / restraint with **100% tie-off** when required (**MCR-049**).  
4. PPE is last line (**MCR-037**).

## 4. Tag interaction

- **Red:** incomplete — scaffolders only.  
- **Yellow:** restricted — harness mandatory for users.  
- **Green:** intended complete protection for duty.
