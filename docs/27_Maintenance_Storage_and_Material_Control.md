# Section 27 — Maintenance, Storage & Material Control

**Status:** Production draft v0.5  

## Couplers

- Free-running lightly lubricated nuts/bolts; examine before use.  
- No heat exposure (**MCR-014**).  
- Segregate defective fittings — quarantine bin.  

## Tubes & boards

- Reject defective tubes; never cut on site without supervisor (**MCR-038**).  
- Boards: marks for max span; defect cut-back only if remaining compliant (**MCR-026**).  
- Sole boards stored separately from deck boards (**MCR-007**).  

## Storage

- Stacks stable; off soft mud; protected from vehicle damage.  
- Tags/forms controlled stationery.  

## Gin wheels & rope

- SWL marked; inspect rope/sheave; GAP-014 procedure still thin.

## Honest gaps

No Anabeeb material scrap rates or NDT regime for tubes.
