## 1. Glossary (working)

| # | Term | Working definition | Deck note |
|---|------|--------------------|-----------|
| 1 | **Post / standard** | Vertical tube carrying platform and loads to ground | s30 |
| 2 | **Kicker lift** | First low lift near ground | Max **150 mm** from ground — MCR-022 |
| 3 | **Ledger** | Horizontal member parallel to façade (longitudinal) | s31 |
| 4 | **Access ladder** | Means of access/egress | 4:1 slope — MCR-023 |
| 5 | **Transom** | Horizontal member across bay (supports boards) | s32 |
| 6 | **Board bearer** | Member supporting boards (related to boarded span) | s32 |
| 8 | **Transverse / section brace** | Brace in plane of bay ends | **35°–55°** from horizontal — MCR-016 |
| 9 | **Longitudinal brace** | Brace along length of scaffold | **35°–55°** — MCR-016 |
| 11 | **Top rail** | Upper edge protection | Anabeeb MCR-021; training MCR-021a |
| 12 | **Mid rail** | Intermediate edge protection | Mid height (training); ≥53 cm (WAH) |
| 13 | **Base plate** | Plate under standard/jack | MCR-003 |
| 14 | **Sole board** | Timber spreading load to ground | MCR-005–007 |

## 2. Related terms (expanded)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Node point** | Intersection of members where braces/ties should connect near |
| **Bay** | Space between standards along length |
| **Lift** | Vertical storey between ledgers/platforms |
| **Facade / face bracing** | Bracing on face of independent scaffold |
| **Ledger bracing** | Bracing on ledger lines (alternate lines — MCR-020) |
| **Tie** | Connection to permanent structure (Ch11) |
| **Scaff-tag** | Status indicator at access (Ch17) |
| **Duty class** | Platform service load class (Ch09) |
| **Raker** | Inclined support tube (cantilever) |
| **Trapeze** | Assist tube for underhung work (≤600 mm from ledger) |

## 3. System-language caution

Cuplok/Ringlock use manufacturer terms (ledgers, standards, diagonals, decks) that map conceptually but not always 1:1 to tube-and-fitting coupler language.
