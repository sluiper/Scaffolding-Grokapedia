## 1. Weather stop-work (WAH)

Stop work at height for: strong wind (**number GAP-009**), rain/slippery, sandstorm, poor visibility, lightning, adverse thermal. [CITATION: WAH-P010]

## 2. Heat (KSA)

Link planned **AIMS-L3-HSE-SWP-013 Heat Stress** — not yet extracted (**GAP-020**). Practical: hydration, rest, shade; PPE heat load on scaffolders.

## 3. SIMOPS interfaces

| Interface | Risk | Control sketch |
|-----------|------|----------------|
| Crane / lifting | Struck, overload, swing | Exclusion, radio, gin wheel discipline |
| Excavation | Undermine bases | GAP-016 — coordinate excavation SWP |
| Hot work | Fire, damage couplers/heat | No heat on couplers (MCR-014) |
| Painting / grit | Overload boards, slip | Duty class + housekeeping |
| Public / process plant | Drop, access | Barricade, tags, client PTW |

## 4. Environmental

Housekeeping waste; prevent dropped objects to process areas; sole boards not left as trip hazards after demob.
