## 3. Hazard list (training deck)

Fall of personnel; fall of equipment/tools; defective equipment; slip/trip/fall same level; chemicals; contact with moving/stationary equipment; manual handling; vehicles/plant/cranes. [CITATION: PPTX-M1 s7]

## 4. PPE hierarchy

PPE is the **last line of defence**. Prefer:

1. Eliminate / avoid work at height  
2. Collective protection (guardrails, nets, complete platforms)  
3. Administrative (permits, tags, exclusion)  
4. PPE (harness, lanyard, helmet, etc.)  

[CITATION: PPTX-M1 s8 + WAH hierarchy]

**Yellow-tag scaffolds:** harness & lanyard mandatory for users (MCR-028 / PPTX s62).

## 6. Weather stop-work

Stop work at height for adverse weather: strong wind (**numeric threshold missing — GAP-009**), rain/slippery surfaces, sandstorms, poor visibility, lightning, adverse thermal. [CITATION: WAH-P010]

## 7. Scaffold as temporary platform

Scaffolding, mobile and suspended scaffolds are recognised temporary platforms under WAH (MCR-052). They do not exempt the duty to inspect, tag, and control access.
