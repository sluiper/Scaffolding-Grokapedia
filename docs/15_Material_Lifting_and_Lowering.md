## 1. Gin wheel (Module-1)

Scaffold materials lifted to elevations using a **gin wheel**.

| Limit | Value | MCR |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Max SWL | **50 kg only** | 031 |
| Max horizontal tube outreach | **750 mm** | 031 |
| Fixing to scaffold | **2 right-angle couplers** | 031 |

[CITATION: PPTX-M1 s54]

**GAP-014:** Only PPTX; no Anabeeb SWP confirmation. Treat as Drafting training limit until SWP-019/lifting procedure aligns.

## 2. Control measures

1. Know SWL — never exceed 50 kg on this training gin-wheel rule.  
2. Control **drop zone** below (**MCR-048**); barricade and signs.  
3. Competent person operating; clear communication.  
4. Do not use defective rope/wheel.  
5. Materials not carried on ladders by climbers (**MCR-061**) — lift separately.
