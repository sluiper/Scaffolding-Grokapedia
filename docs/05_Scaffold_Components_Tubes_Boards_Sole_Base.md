## 1. Tubes

### 1.1 Dimensions [CITATION + EN39-sec]

| System | OD | Wall | Max length (deck) | Standard cited in deck |
|--------|-----|------|-------------------|------------------------|
| Tube & fitting standards | 48.3 mm | **4.0 mm** | 6 m | BS 1139 / EN 39 |
| Cuplok | 48.3 mm | **3.2 mm** | 6 m | EN 10219 (deck) |

EN 39 secondary restatement: Type 3 = 3.2 mm; Type 4 = 4.0 mm; OD 48.3 mm; example steel S235GT ReH ≥ 235 MPa. [CITATION secondary]

### 1.2 Operational rules

- Do not use defective tube.  
- **Never cut tube on site** unless instructed by supervisor (**MCR-038**).  

## 2. Base plates, jacks, sole boards

| Item | Rule | MCR |
|------|------|-----|
| Base plate | 150 × 150 × 6 mm | 003 |
| Base jack extension | ≤ **2/3** thread height | 004 |
| Sole board min | 38 × 225 × 450 mm | 005 |
| Omit sole board | asphalt or concrete only | 006 |
| Sole ≠ scaffold board | never return sole boards to board service | 007 |
| Shared sole (double standard) | 750 × 225 × 75 mm **or** two 450 × 225 × 38 mm central | 027 |

Sole boards spread load to ground. Double soles for special/load-bearing cases (deck).

## 3. Scaffold boards / decks

- Metal decking units **or** timber/LVL boards.  
- LVL / timber to **BS 2482**; nominal **38 mm × 225 mm** (**MCR-026**).  
- Marking: standard name, manufacturer trade name, **maximum span**.  
- Defect boards out of service; damaged sections may be cut back only if remaining part remains compliant (deck).  

**GAP-011:** PPTX text “225m wide” is a typo for **225 mm** (**MCR-065**). “OSHA proof tested 1.8mm” is ambiguous — treat as **unverified**; do not teach 1.8 mm as a dimension.

## 4. Ladder beams (prefabricated)

For engineering / load-bearing / bridge scaffold: BS EN 39 Type 4 tubes 48.3 mm, 4 mm wall, fy ≥ 235 N/mm² (**MCR-024**).  
Splice good practice: short tubes top & bottom chords with min **4 swivels + 1 sleeve** per chord end (**MCR-025**). Bad practice: sleeve-only chord joins (deck s59).

## 5. Worked example — sole board selection [DERIVED]

**Given:** Two standards share one sole board on soft ground.  
**Deck rule:** shared sole min **750 × 225 × 75 mm**.  
**Alternative:** individual soles **450 × 225 × 38 mm** each, base plate central, combined plan ~450×450 mm if spacing allows.  

Select shared heavy sole when standards are close and load path shares one timber; use individuals when spacing allows independent bearing.
