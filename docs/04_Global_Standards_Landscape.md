## 1. Purpose of standards in this encyclopedia

Standards define **materials, fittings performance, platform loads, and good practice configurations**. They do not replace Anabeeb or client procedures when those are stricter (**MCR-043 / 044**).

## 5. Proprietary systems

Cuplok / Ringlock / Layher-type nodes follow **manufacturer manuals** in addition to EN 12811 performance concepts (**GAP-022**). Tube OD compatibility with 48.3 fittings does not imply identical bracing rules.

## 6. Engineering outside standard solutions

When configuration leaves TG20/standard sheets (cantilever > 3 m, bridges, special loads, wind-sensitive cladding, etc.) → **MCR-045** design gate.
