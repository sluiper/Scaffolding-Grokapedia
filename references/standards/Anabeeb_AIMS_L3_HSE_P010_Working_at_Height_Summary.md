## Purpose & scope

- Prevent falls and protect ANABEEB employees from fall hazards.
- Applies where risk of falling **1.8 m (6 ft)** or more from unprotected edge or elevated surface.

Anabeeb controlled procedure is the site rule for Anabeeb fall protection. Training deck band overlaps but lower floor is 950 mm (UK WAH min). **Encyclopedia rule:** teach **Anabeeb 1.1 m ± 8 cm** as governing on Anabeeb work; note UK ≥950 mm as international floor.

## Ladder safety (aligned with PPTX)

- Inspect before use  
- **4:1** angle ratio  
- Extend **≥ 1 m** above landing  
- Three-point contact  

## Fall arrest

- 100% tie-off with double lanyards or SRL when working at height (procedure text)  
- Anchorage **≥ 22.2 kN (5000 lb)** per person  
- Hierarchy: prevent with barriers/nets first; then restrict/arrest  

## Weather stop-work

Stop work at height for: strong wind (threshold incomplete in extract — “Strong winds exceeding” cut off), rain/slippery surfaces, sandstorms, poor visibility, lightning, adverse thermal conditions.
